Tsong checks these, in this order, on every token sent to orders.test. A token that fails any of them is refused.

audiences

The token must carry tsong. It may also carry api://orders, and nothing else.

scopes

The token must carry orders.read. It may also carry orders.write, and nothing else. A request may narrow these, but never drop orders.read.

rules

None. Rules come with CEL support.

the minted token

Signed with ES256, key es256-2026-11. Its audience is the inbound audience plus orders.test, and its scopes are the inbound scopes, narrowed by the request.

callers

Any client address may call this route.
