# Code of Conduct

Tsong is a technical project. This code covers how people treat each other in its issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space, and it applies the same way to everyone.

## Expected

- Judge contributions on their technical merit: correctness, security, tests, clarity.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people. Say what's wrong and, where you can, how to fix it.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Stay on topic. Project spaces are for Tsong.
- Accept a maintainer's decision once it's made, or make your case once more with new evidence.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults or threats, against anyone, for any reason.
- Harassment: continuing to contact someone who has asked you to stop, or following someone across spaces to pursue a dispute.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Disclosing a vulnerability publicly before its fix, contrary to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers privately, by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or in a private vulnerability report if that's the only private channel you have. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainers decide what action to take, in proportion to what happened: a warning, removing or editing a comment, locking a thread, or blocking someone from the project, temporarily or for good. The maintainers' decision is final.
